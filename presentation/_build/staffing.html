The one decision that unlocks everything: how to resource it
There’s no in-house technology team today — so someone has to do the work. Three ways to staff it:
BuildHIRE YOUR OWN TEAM
TIME TO REAL PROGRESS3–6 months to recruit & ramp
YOU GETMost direct control; the capability lives fully in-house
TRADE-OFFSlowest to start; hiring risk while billing issues wait
PartnerBRING IN AN EXPERIENCED FIRM
TIME TO REAL PROGRESSFastest start — expertise on day one
YOU GETImmediate delivery capacity without hiring
TRADE-OFFLess in-house ownership unless deliberately transitioned
HybridPARTNER NOW, BUILD OVER TIME
TIME TO REAL PROGRESSFast start, then grow your own team
YOU GETQuick momentum and you end up owning the capability
TRADE-OFFNeeds a clear hand-over plan from day one
THE POINT
This choice gates the entire roadmap. Which option you pick matters less than deciding soon — nothing improves durably until it’s made.
